About

Small studio, handmade everything.

Doctor Paints started with a simple idea: art should feel like it was made for you, not just sold to you. Every print, letter and story piece we make begins with a brush, a pencil, or both — never a template, never AI-generated art.

We paint in watercolour, gouache and ink, then either digitise the piece for instant download or print and frame it ourselves for local, careful delivery. Custom pieces — alphabets, motivational words, and full "Story in a Frame" commissions — are designed one at a time, in conversation with you.

Based in India, shipping pan-India, and hand-delivering framed pieces via Porter across a growing list of cities.
