FAQ

Shipping, Porter delivery & printing

How does Porter delivery work?

What sizes are available?

All ready-made prints and word art come in A4 (21 x 29.7 cm) and A3 (29.7 x 42 cm). Custom "Story in a Frame" pieces can be made to any size on request.

Can I print the digital download myself?

Yes — see our full paper & printing guide. In short: 200–300 GSM matte or cotton paper at 300 DPI gives the truest result.

How long do custom pieces take?

Custom alphabets take 3–10 days depending on tier. "Story in a Frame" pieces start with a concept sketch within 2–3 days, then 7–14 days to complete after you approve it.

Do you ship outside the Porter cities?

Yes — anywhere in India via a tracked courier at a flat ₹149 for printed or framed pieces. Digital downloads are delivered by email instantly, anywhere in the world.

How do I pay?

We confirm your order and final price (including any delivery charge) on WhatsApp, then share a UPI / payment link before we begin.
